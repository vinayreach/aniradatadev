FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our services and how we work. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Working together

What does Anira Data DevOps do?

We provide finance and accounting operations, global payroll support, ERP consulting, finance transformation and automation, software development, cloud and DevOps services, and IT and finance talent.

Where is your team based?

Our delivery center is in Madhapur, Hyderabad, India.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many engagements begin with a single process or a defined project and expand once the first one is running well.

How do engagements usually start?

With a conversation about your priorities, followed by a short discovery to agree scope, approach and service standards.

Finance operations

Which finance processes can you support?

Travel and expense, accounts payable, accounts receivable and order-to-cash, record-to-report, financial planning and analysis, business and financial reporting, and global payroll.

Do you work in our systems?

Yes. We work in your ERP and accounting systems under the access and approval rules you define.

Who approves payments and journals?

You do. We prepare, check and document; approval and release authority stay with your designated approvers.

Technology

Which ERP platforms do you work with?

SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Odoo and QuickBooks Enterprise.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. We design, build and support custom applications, integrations and automation, as fixed-scope projects or through dedicated teams.

Do you provide DevOps and database support?

Yes. We build and run CI/CD pipelines and support Oracle databases, including performance tuning and high availability.
