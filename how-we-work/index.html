How we work

Structured, transparent and built around your controls.

Whether we run a finance process, deliver a project or provide talent, every engagement follows the same principles: clear scope, documented processes, regular reporting and continuous improvement.

Engagement models

Four ways to work with us

Choose the model that fits the work. Many clients combine them as the relationship grows.

  • 01

    Managed operations

    We run a defined finance process end to end, such as accounts payable or reconciliations, against agreed service standards.

    Best forOngoing, repeatable work

  • 02

    Project

    A defined outcome and timeline, such as an ERP rollout, an automation or a reporting build.

    Best forOne-off change

  • 03

    Dedicated team

    A stable team that works on your priorities and grows with you.

    Best forLong-term capacity

  • 04

    Talent

    Skilled IT or finance professionals who join your team for a project or ongoing role.

    Best forSpecific skills

Transition

How we take over a process, safely

A step-by-step handover that protects quality and continuity at every stage.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Walk through each process with your team. Agree scope, volumes, systems, controls and success measures.

  2. 02

    Documentation

    Write step-by-step procedures, checklists and escalation paths, and have them signed off by you.

  3. 03

    Parallel run

    Work alongside your team on live transactions, with every output reviewed until quality is consistent.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Take ownership against the agreed service standards, with your team reviewing results.

  5. 05

    Steady state

    Run the process, report against service standards and look for improvements each month.

Governance

A rhythm of communication you can rely on.

You always know what is happening, what is coming up and how the service is performing.

Daily

Work queues, exceptions and urgent items, handled through an agreed channel.

Weekly

Progress, open issues and upcoming deadlines with your process owner.

Monthly

Service report against agreed standards, plus improvement opportunities.

Quarterly

Business review of scope, priorities and the roadmap for the next quarter.

Quality and controls

How we protect quality and your data

  • Documented procedures

    Every process runs from written, approved procedures that are kept up to date.

  • Maker-checker review

    Work that affects your books is prepared by one person and reviewed by another.

  • Access by role

    Team members get only the system access their role needs, as approved by you.

  • Exception logs

    Issues and exceptions are recorded, tracked and reported, never fixed silently.

  • Named contacts

    A named lead and backup for every engagement, so continuity never depends on one person.

  • Continuous improvement

    Regular reviews of errors, delays and effort to find what to fix or automate next.
