The challenges we solve

  • Claims arrive late, incomplete or without receipts

  • Slow reimbursements frustrate employees
  • Corporate card transactions sit unreconciled
  • Little visibility of spend by team, project or category

What we do

Scope of service

Claim review and verification
Checking every claim for receipts, coding and completeness before it moves forward.
Policy compliance checks
Applying your rules on limits, categories and approvals consistently, and recording exceptions.
Corporate travel expense administration
Handling travel-related costs, advances and settlements in line with your travel policy.
Corporate card reconciliation
Matching card transactions to claims and receipts, and following up on gaps.
Reimbursement preparation
Preparing approved claims for payment in your payroll or payables cycle.
Expense reporting and reconciliation
Reconciling expense accounts and reporting spend by cost center and category.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to change our expense tool?

No. We work within your existing expense system and approval workflow, and adapt our process to it.

How are policy exceptions handled?

Exceptions are flagged with supporting notes and routed to the approver your policy names. We apply your policy; we do not override it.

Can you help us improve our expense policy?

Yes. Because we see every exception, we can highlight recurring gaps and unclear rules that are worth addressing in your next policy update.
