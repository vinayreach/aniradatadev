Sound familiar?

The challenges we solve

  • Payroll inputs arriving late or incomplete
  • Different processes and calendars in each country
  • Payroll that does not reconcile to the general ledger
  • Limited consolidated view of payroll costs
  • Pressure to stay on top of changing compliance requirements

What we do

Scope of service

Payroll input management
Collecting and validating joiners, leavers, changes and variable pay.
Payroll processing support
Supporting each pay cycle within your payroll systems and providers.
Multi-country coordination
Keeping calendars, inputs and approvals aligned across countries.
Payroll reconciliation
Reconciling registers, payments and ledger postings each cycle.
Payroll reporting
Consolidated payroll cost reporting by country, entity and department.
Compliance-focused operations
Documented processes and checks that support statutory requirements.

FAQ

Common questions

Which countries do you support?

It depends on where your teams are. Tell us your footprint and we will discuss how we can support it, working with your existing payroll systems and providers.

How is payroll data kept confidential?

Access is limited to named team members, data is handled within your systems wherever possible, and handling rules are agreed with you during transition.

Do you replace our payroll provider?

Not necessarily. Many clients keep their providers and use us to coordinate inputs, review outputs and reconcile results.
