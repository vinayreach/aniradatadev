The challenges we solve

  • Invoices that go out late or with errors
  • Payments received but not applied to the right invoices
  • Collections follow-up that depends on who has time
  • Disputes that linger without an owner
  • No clear picture of customer credit risk

What we do

Scope of service

Billing and customer invoicing
Accurate, timely invoices issued in line with contracts and orders.
Cash application
Matching receipts to invoices and clearing unapplied cash quickly.
Collections
Structured, courteous follow-up based on aging and customer priority.
Dispute management
Logging, routing and tracking disputes through to resolution.
Credit risk monitoring
Watching payment behavior and limits to flag risk early.
AR analytics and DSO tracking
Aging, DSO and collection trends that show where cash is stuck.

What we track

Measures we report

  • Days sales outstanding (DSO)How quickly invoices turn into cash.
  • Aging profileBalances by age bucket and customer.
  • Unapplied cashReceipts not yet matched to invoices.
  • Dispute resolution timeHow long disputes stay open.

FAQ

Common questions

Will you contact our customers?

Only if you want us to. Many clients prefer we prepare worklists and correspondence while their team owns the relationship; others ask us to handle routine follow-up. We agree this up front.

Can you help reduce DSO?

We focus on the drivers of DSO: accurate invoicing, prompt cash application, consistent follow-up and faster dispute resolution, and we report on them regularly.

Do you support multiple currencies and entities?

Yes. We can support receivables across entities and currencies within your systems.
