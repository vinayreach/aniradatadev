FAQ

Common questions

Do you build dashboards in our BI tool?

Yes. We work with the reporting and BI tools you already use, so there is nothing new to buy or learn.

How do you make sure the numbers are right?

Reported figures are reconciled to the ledger and source systems before they are published, and definitions are documented.

Can reporting be automated?

Often, yes. Once definitions and data sources are stable, we look for ways to automate data preparation and refresh.
