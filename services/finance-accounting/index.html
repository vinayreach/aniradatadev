FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

Can we start with just one process?

Yes. Many clients start with a single process such as accounts payable or reconciliations, and extend once the first one is running smoothly.

Do you work in our systems?

Yes. We work in your ERP and accounting systems under the access and approval rules you define.

How do you hand over work from our team?

Through a structured transition: we document each process, work alongside your team, then take over with agreed checks and reporting. See How We Work for details.
